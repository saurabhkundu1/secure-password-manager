Secure Pass is an offline, high-security encrypted password manager for Android. Store your credentials safely in a local, encrypted vault using AES-256-GCM, unlocked via a 6-digit PIN or Biometrics.

Features:
- Total Privacy: 100% offline. No internet permissions required.
- Military-Grade Encryption: AES-256-GCM encryption with keys derived via PBKDF2.
- Secure Access: Mandatory 6-digit master code.
- Biometric Integration: Fingerprint support for convenience.
- Personalized UI: 9 dynamic color palettes with full support for Light and Dark modes.
- Password Generator: Create strong, customizable passwords.
- Secure Notes: Store additional details for each account.
- Encrypted Backups: Export and import your vault as encrypted text files.

Your data never leaves your device. No cloud, no trackers, just your passwords.
