Secure Pass is an offline, high-security encrypted password manager for Android. Store your credentials safely in a local, encrypted vault using AES-256-GCM, unlocked via a 6-digit PIN or Biometrics.

Key Features:
- 🔒 Total Privacy: 100% offline. Zero internet permissions, zero tracking.
- 🛡️ Military-Grade Encryption: AES-256-GCM encryption with keys derived via PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations).
- 🔢 Secure Access: Mandatory 6-digit master PIN.
- 🖐️ Biometric Support: Fingerprint unlock with 24-hour PIN re-entry policy.
- 🎨 Personalized UI: 13 dynamic color palettes with full support for Light and Dark modes.
- 🔍 Search & Favorites: Instantly filter entries or pin important accounts.
- 🎲 Password Utilities: Built-in generator and real-time strength indicator.
- 📁 Encrypted Backups: Securely export and import your vault as encrypted text files.

Your data never leaves your device. Secure, private, and simple.
